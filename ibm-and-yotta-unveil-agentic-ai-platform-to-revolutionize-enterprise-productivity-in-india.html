IBM and Yotta launch an agentic AI platform in India

Published 7 May 2026 by Web Online Tools Editorial

IBM and Yotta launched an agentic AI platform for Indian businesses and government, focused on security and local compliance.

In a significant move poised to reshape the landscape of artificial intelligence adoption within the enterprise sector, IBM and Yotta Data Services Private Limited today announced plans for a strategic partnership. This collaboration aims to deliver an advanced Agentic AI Platform specifically tailored for businesses and government organizations across India. The initiative signals a robust step forward in operationalizing AI, moving beyond experimental stages to practical, secure, and compliant deployment across critical business functions.

The announcement underscores a growing demand for AI solutions that not only enhance productivity but also adhere to stringent requirements around data residency, security, and local regulatory compliance. As organizations increasingly seek to leverage AI for complex, multi-step tasks, the need for platforms that can intelligently orchestrate these processes while maintaining rigorous governance has become paramount.

Who is Behind This Agentic AI Push?

The partnership brings together the global technological prowess of IBM and the robust sovereign cloud infrastructure provided by Yotta Data Services Private Limited. IBM, a long-standing leader in enterprise technology, is contributing its cutting-edge AI capabilities, particularly through its watsonx Orchestrate platform. Yotta, a prominent Indian provider of sovereign cloud infrastructure and platform services, is deploying this innovative solution on its Shakti Cloud, ensuring data sovereignty and high-performance computing.

What is an Agentic AI Platform?

At its core, the proposed platform is designed to enable organizations to deploy and manage AI agents across a wide array of business functions. Unlike simpler AI tools that perform isolated tasks, an agentic AI system is capable of understanding context, making decisions, and executing multi-step workflows autonomously or semi-autonomously. For enterprises, this means AI can take on more complex, end-to-end operations in areas like IT service management, human resources (HR), finance, procurement, and customer support.

The platform will leverage IBM watsonx Orchestrate, a powerful engine designed to structure and manage these multi-step AI operations. Integrating this with Yotta's Shakti Cloud provides a secure, scalable, and locally compliant environment essential for Indian enterprises and government bodies. Furthermore, the collaboration intends to bring IBM Sovereign Core to India, a software platform that helps organizations build and operate AI-ready sovereign environments with continuous compliance monitoring and governed AI execution.

When and Where Will This Platform Be Available?

The plans for this strategic partnership were formally announced today, May 7, 2026, from Bengaluru, India. The platform is specifically targeted at enterprises and government organizations within India, aiming to address their unique requirements for digital transformation while upholding national data security and privacy standards.

Why This Agentic AI Platform Matters for Indian Enterprises

The core motivation behind this collaboration is to enable Indian organizations to scale AI adoption responsibly. As Sandip Patel, Managing Director of IBM India & South Asia, emphasized, "Indian enterprises are increasingly focused on operationalising AI in a way that is secure, governed, and aligned with regulatory expectations." This platform addresses this by embedding sovereignty, governance, transparency, and trust from the outset.

  • Accelerated AI Adoption: Provides a clear path for organizations to move from AI experimentation to full operational deployment.
  • Enhanced Productivity: Automates and optimizes complex workflows across diverse departments, freeing up human resources for strategic tasks.
  • Data Sovereignty and Compliance: Ensures that data processing and storage adhere to Indian regulations, a critical concern for both public and private sectors.
  • Secure AI Execution: Offers a robust, secure environment for deploying sensitive AI applications, protecting against cyber risks.

How the Agentic AI Platform Will Transform Workflows

The new Agentic AI Platform is set to transform how businesses operate by integrating AI directly into their core processes. Imagine AI agents handling routine IT support tickets from start to finish, onboarding new employees by coordinating tasks across HR and IT systems, or even managing complex procurement processes by identifying vendors, negotiating terms, and processing orders. This goes beyond simple automation; it involves intelligent agents that can adapt and learn.

Why This Matters

This partnership marks a significant milestone in the global AI landscape, particularly for emerging economies like India. By focusing on a compliant, secure, and sovereign Agentic AI Platform, IBM and Yotta are addressing fundamental concerns that often hinder large-scale AI deployment. This collaboration is not just about technology; it's about building trust in AI, ensuring ethical deployment, and providing a competitive edge through responsible innovation. For webonlinetools.com, it signifies the evolution of online tools from standalone utilities to interconnected, intelligent agents that drive unprecedented levels of productivity and operational efficiency in a globally connected yet locally governed digital environment.

"AI innovation in India must be anchored in sovereignty, security, and performance. Together with IBM, we propose to enable enterprises to harness the power of agentic AI on a secure, compliant and sovereign cloud infrastructure," said Sunil Gupta, Co-Founder, MD & CEO, Yotta Data Services.

What's Next for the Agentic AI Platform

Moving forward, IBM and Yotta intend to jointly pursue various go-to-market initiatives, including solution co-creation, proof of concepts, and technical enablement. Their efforts will target key sectors such as Banking, Financial Services and Insurance (BFSI), public sector, manufacturing, and digital-native industries. This strategic rollout will likely set a precedent for how large-scale, compliant Enterprise AI solutions are deployed in other regions, emphasizing the critical balance between innovation and governance in the rapidly evolving world of artificial intelligence.
