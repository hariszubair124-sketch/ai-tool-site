Google Cloud and Randstad use AI agents for staff onboarding

Google Cloud and Randstad Digital are using AI agents to capture team knowledge and get new employees up to speed faster.

12 June 20264 min readBy Web Online Tools Editorial

In a significant stride for artificial intelligence applications in real-world business challenges, Google Cloud and Randstad Digital have announced a groundbreaking collaboration. This partnership introduces an advanced agentic AI solution, named Forze Mirate, specifically designed to tackle the critical issue of knowledge transfer and accelerated onboarding in high-turnover environments. Unveiled on June 11, 2026, this innovative deployment leverages the power of Google Cloud’s Gemini Enterprise and Gemini models to enhance productivity and streamline operations, offering a compelling case study for the burgeoning field of agentic AI.

The Challenge: Bridging the Knowledge Gap in Dynamic Teams

Many organizations grapple with the constant influx and outflow of talent, particularly in specialized fields where institutional knowledge is paramount. The Forze Hydrogen Racing team, a world-renowned student motorsport collective focused on building hydrogen-powered race cars, faces this challenge acutely. Each year, the team experiences a complete workforce turnover, onboarding 50 to 60 new student engineers who must quickly assimilate complex engineering projects and a vast repository of historical data.

Over 18 years, Forze's technical DNA—encompassing specifications, CAD drawings, telemetry, and operational guidelines—had become fragmented across thousands of documents and multiple platforms. This dispersed knowledge created a steep learning curve for new members, making them heavily reliant on alumni and lead engineers to answer repetitive, foundational queries. Such friction led to valuable time loss and hampered immediate productivity. This scenario is a concentrated version of a challenge many enterprises face, highlighting the widespread problem of losing critical institutional knowledge when talent rotates.

Forze Mirate: An Agentic AI Solution in Action

Addressing this precise pain point, Randstad Digital, a leading partner in digital and AI talent solutions, developed Forze Mirate. Built natively on Google Cloud’s secure infrastructure, this agentic AI solution acts as an intelligent knowledge conduit. It synthesizes vast amounts of decentralized historical data into an accessible, conversational platform, providing real-time, actionable insights to new engineers.

How Forze Mirate Works

At its core, Forze Mirate utilizes Google Cloud's powerful AI stack, including Gemini Enterprise. Engineers can query 18 years of engineering history using natural language, receiving contextualized, role-specific answers. Crucially, the system provides citations to the original documents, ensuring transparency and trustworthiness. The platform's capabilities extend beyond text; it processes visual context, allowing engineers to upload images of complex wiring and instantly retrieve relevant technical specifications and safety instructions. This multimodal understanding is key to its effectiveness in a highly visual and technical domain.

  • Natural Language Querying: Users interact with the system using everyday language, making complex information accessible.
  • Contextualized Responses: Answers are tailored to the engineer's specific role and the query's context.
  • Source Citations: Every piece of information is traceable back to its original document, building trust and verification.
  • Multimodal Processing: The ability to interpret visual data, such as images of wiring, and link it to relevant documentation significantly enhances utility.

Transformative Impact on Productivity and Workflow

The deployment of Forze Mirate has yielded remarkable results for Forze Hydrogen Racing. The team has witnessed a significant transformation in its efficiency and operational autonomy. According to the official announcement, the time-to-productivity for new engineers has been reduced by up to three times. This acceleration is a direct consequence of eliminating the steep learning curve traditionally associated with joining the team.

Furthermore, the reliance on alumni and lead engineers for foundational knowledge has decreased by an impressive 80%. This minimizes technical interruptions for experienced members, allowing them to focus on higher-value tasks and innovation rather than repetitive knowledge transfer. The solution has also improved institutional knowledge retention by 50%, ensuring that valuable engineering breakthroughs and lessons learned are preserved across generations of the team. This demonstrates the profound impact of agentic AI on streamlining workflows and preserving critical expertise.

"What Forze Hydrogen Racing faces on an annual basis is a concentrated version of a challenge many enterprises face: the loss of critical institutional knowledge when talent turns over," said Peter Kouwen, Director, Randstad Digital, The Netherlands. "By combining Randstad Digital's talent expertise with Google Cloud's generative AI, we aren't just solving a data problem; we are also fundamentally empowering human talent. AI’s true value is unlocked…"

Why This Matters for Modern Businesses and Online Productivity Tools

The success of Forze Mirate offers a powerful blueprint for enterprises across all sectors. The efficient management and transfer of institutional knowledge are perennial challenges that directly impact operational efficiency, innovation cycles, and employee satisfaction. This agentic AI solution demonstrates how advanced AI, when applied strategically, can unlock significant productivity gains and ensure business continuity, even with dynamic workforces. For users of webonlinetools.com, this highlights a critical trend: AI is moving beyond generic chatbots to specialized, context-aware agents that directly augment human capabilities and optimize workflows.

The emphasis on secure infrastructure and detailed citations within Forze Mirate also underscores the growing importance of trusted and verifiable AI solutions. As businesses increasingly integrate AI into their core operations, the ability to trace information and ensure data integrity becomes non-negotiable. This development showcases that practical, ethical, and highly effective AI applications are already here, driving tangible results in demanding technical environments.

What's Next for Agentic AI and Knowledge Management

The collaboration between Google Cloud and Randstad Digital points to a future where agentic AI systems become indispensable components of enterprise knowledge management and productivity strategies. We can expect to see similar AI agents deployed across various industries, from manufacturing and healthcare to legal and financial services, where complex, constantly evolving knowledge bases are critical. The continued evolution of multimodal AI will further enhance these systems, allowing them to interpret and act upon an even wider range of data types, including video and real-time sensor data. As AI becomes more deeply embedded in operational workflows, expect online tools for collaboration, documentation, and training to integrate these agentic capabilities, transforming how teams learn, share, and achieve their goals.
