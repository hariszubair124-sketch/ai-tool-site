Revolutionizing Product Development: NTT DATA's AI Consumer Agents Slash Research Time by 99%

Published 19 March 2026 by Web Online Tools Editorial

Discover how NTT DATA's innovative AI Consumer Agents are revolutionizing product development for Kao, drastically cutting market research time by 99% and setting a new standard for business agility and efficiency with AI-powered insights.

In a groundbreaking announcement today, March 19, 2026, NTT DATA, a global leader in AI, digital business, and technology services, revealed the successful proof of concept (PoC) of its "AI Consumers" for Kao Corporation, a prominent Japanese consumer goods company. This significant development marks a pivotal moment in the application of artificial intelligence, demonstrating its immense potential to enhance business productivity and efficiency. The project showcases how AI Consumer Agents can dramatically accelerate product development research, achieving an unprecedented 99% reduction in timeline.

The successful PoC, conducted for one of Kao's makeup brands, assessed the feasibility of leveraging AI models to simulate consumer behavioral characteristics and develop accurate personas. This innovative approach aims to streamline and deepen product development research, a critical need in today's fast-paced market.

Understanding AI Consumer Agents in Market Research

At its core, AI Consumer Agents are sophisticated AI models designed to mimic human consumers. They draw upon vast datasets, including accumulated consumer research, purchase histories, and social media activity, to simulate diverse behavioral patterns and preferences. By creating these digital personas, companies can essentially conduct comprehensive market research and consumer interviews without the traditional constraints of time and resources.

NTT DATA's marketing AI agent service was instrumental in this process, working in conjunction with Kao's extensive proprietary data. This synergy allowed for the creation of multiple AI Consumers and even an AI interviewer, enabling a dynamic and in-depth research environment that mirrored real-world interactions.

The Challenge: Responding to Rapidly Shifting Consumer Trends

The consumer goods industry, particularly in sectors like cosmetics, operates at an incredibly high velocity. Consumer trends, which historically shifted quarterly or monthly, now evolve on a weekly cycle. For brands like Kao, launching new products each season, the ability to respond with agility and precision to these dynamic trends is paramount for business growth.

Traditional product development research processes are often time-consuming and costly, involving extensive efforts in research design, respondent recruitment, fieldwork, and data analysis. These conventional methods make it challenging to sufficiently deepen research data or validate hypotheses within tight development timelines, often leading to missed opportunities or slower market entry for new products.

How NTT DATA's Innovation Delivers Unprecedented Insights

NTT DATA addressed this pressing industry challenge by deploying its advanced marketing AI agent service. By combining this service with Kao's rich repository of consumer research, purchase, and social media data, the team successfully generated multiple AI Consumer Agents and an AI interviewer.

This AI-powered market research methodology allowed Kao to gain insights consistent with those obtained through traditional research methods. However, the most significant impact was on efficiency. The PoC demonstrated that AI-driven research could shorten the product development research timeline from approximately 1.5 months to a mere 0.5 days. This astounding 99% reduction represents a monumental leap in operational efficiency and enables a more sophisticated consumer-insight process.

Why This Matters for Business Agility and Innovation

The success of NTT DATA's AI Consumer Agents highlights a transformative shift in how businesses can approach market research and product development. This innovation is crucial for several reasons:

  • Unprecedented Speed to Market: Drastically reduced research timelines mean companies can develop and launch products much faster, capitalizing on fleeting consumer trends and gaining a significant competitive edge.
  • Enhanced Agility: Businesses can iterate on product ideas and marketing strategies with greater frequency and flexibility, adapting quickly to market feedback and emerging needs.
  • Reduced Operational Costs: Automating large portions of the research process, from respondent recruitment to data collection and preliminary analysis, significantly lowers operational workloads and associated costs.
  • Deeper, Faster Insights: AI can process and analyze vast quantities of data far more quickly than human researchers, potentially uncovering nuanced patterns and insights that might otherwise be missed.
  • Strategic Resource Allocation: By offloading routine research tasks to AI, human teams can focus on higher-value activities such as strategic planning, creative development, and complex problem-solving.

This advancement perfectly aligns with the mission of webonlinetools.com, demonstrating how cutting-edge AI can function as a powerful productivity tool, fundamentally changing business workflows and enabling unprecedented levels of efficiency and innovation.

What's Next for AI-Powered Product Development

NTT DATA plans to expand the application of these impressive results beyond just product development and into broader marketing operations. The company is actively promoting both proactive AI adoption and responsible governance to drive business transformation for its clients and within its own operations. Their vision includes realizing "Smart AI Agent®" systems, where AI agents autonomously extract, organize, and execute tasks based on user instructions, further enhancing productivity.

The long-term impact of such AI Consumer Agents could lead to AI-native business process transformation, helping to address societal challenges like labor shortages and enabling a future where individuals can dedicate their efforts to more value-added activities. As AI continues to evolve, we can expect to see similar transformative applications emerge across various industries, making traditional methods increasingly obsolete and paving the way for a new era of innovation and efficiency.
