Marvell shows 2nm optical interconnect for AI data centers

Published 21 September 2026 by Web Online Tools Editorial

Marvell presented a 2nm optical interconnect at ECOC 2026, aimed at faster, more energy-efficient links between AI servers.

Today, September 21, 2026, marks a pivotal moment in the evolution of artificial intelligence, as Marvell Technology, Inc. announced groundbreaking advancements in optical interconnect solutions. These industry-first 2nm optical technology demonstrations are poised to redefine the capabilities and efficiency of AI data center infrastructure, addressing the exponential demands of modern AI workloads. This innovation, showcased at the European Conference on Optical Communication (ECOC) 2026, promises faster, more energy-efficient communication pathways crucial for the next generation of AI development and deployment.

Who is Marvell and What Did They Announce?

Marvell Technology, Inc. (NASDAQ: MRVL) stands as a global leader in data infrastructure semiconductor solutions, consistently pushing the boundaries of connectivity and processing. At ECOC 2026, held in Málaga, Spain, from September 20–24, 2026, Marvell presented its pioneering 2nm optical interconnect demonstrations. This significant announcement underscores Marvell’s commitment to delivering advanced connectivity solutions vital for scaling AI infrastructure. The showcased technologies are specifically optimized to enable ultra-high-speed interconnects, facilitating quicker and more efficient data exchange between XPUs, CPUs, accelerators, and memory resources within data centers.

The company highlighted several key demonstrations, including the first 2nm 400G/lane optical PAM4 technology, which provides low-power, ultra-high-speed optical connections essential for transitioning to 3.2T data center connectivity. Furthermore, Marvell unveiled 2nm 800G ZR/ZR+ pluggable solutions with MACsec, and 2nm 1.6T ZR and O-band coherent-lite technologies, all designed to meet the rigorous demands of scaling AI systems. These advancements collectively signal a profound shift in how AI-driven data centers will manage their ever-increasing power and bandwidth requirements.

The 2nm Breakthrough: Powering Next-Gen AI Computing

The transition to 2nm process technology for optical components represents a monumental leap in semiconductor design, offering unprecedented levels of performance and integration. Marvell's 2nm 400G/lane optical PAM4 demonstration is particularly significant, as it showcases the ability to transmit data at incredibly high speeds while minimizing power consumption. This is critical for AI data center infrastructure, where every watt saved and every nanosecond gained contributes to overall efficiency and computational prowess. The underlying principle involves optimizing the communication pathways within the data center, ensuring that the vast amounts of data generated and processed by AI models can move freely and without bottlenecks.

Beyond raw speed, the emphasis is also on reducing the energy footprint. As AI models grow in complexity and size, the power required to train and run them escalates dramatically. Marvell’s focus on energy efficiency means that data center operators can manage these increasing power demands more effectively, leading to lower operational costs and a reduced environmental impact. This dual focus on speed and efficiency is a hallmark of truly transformative technology in the AI hardware space.

Why This Matters for AI Data Center Infrastructure

The ramifications of Marvell's 2nm optical technology for AI data center infrastructure are far-reaching. Firstly, it directly addresses the critical need for scalability. Modern AI, from large language models to complex simulation environments, requires immense computational resources that are constantly expanding. This new technology enables data centers to scale from 1.6T to 3.2T and beyond, accommodating more powerful AI accelerators and larger datasets without compromising performance. This scalability ensures that AI research and deployment can continue at an accelerated pace, driving innovation across various industries.

Secondly, improved performance translates directly into faster AI training times and more responsive AI applications. This means that developers at companies relying on advanced online tools, for instance, can iterate more quickly on new features powered by AI, delivering more sophisticated and efficient solutions to users. The ability to rapidly process and analyze data is paramount for tasks ranging from real-time analytics to complex scientific simulations, making these interconnects an invisible yet indispensable backbone for almost any advanced digital service.

Finally, the focus on energy efficiency is not merely an economic consideration but a strategic one. As the world grapples with climate change and energy constraints, building sustainable AI infrastructure is paramount. Marvell's advanced optical and silicon design leverages leading-edge process nodes to deliver significant improvements in power per bit, making AI operations more environmentally responsible and economically viable in the long term. This also helps align with the broader industry push towards green computing initiatives.

How Marvell's Innovation Works

Marvell's breakthrough stems from its sophisticated approach to optical and silicon design, integrating advanced Co-Packaged Optics (CPO) technology. The CPO platform directly integrates optics with AI accelerators and switch silicon, which is a crucial development as per-lane speeds and multi-rack AI pods begin to push past the traditional reach limits of copper cabling. This integration drastically reduces the distance data needs to travel between electrical and optical components, thereby minimizing signal loss and power consumption, while maximizing bandwidth density.

Demonstrations at ECOC 2026 showcased the platform achieving an astounding 102.4 Tbps with 200G-per-lane silicon photonics. This level of performance is achieved by leveraging the finest 2nm process nodes, which allows for the creation of incredibly compact and efficient components. By building these advanced optical solutions directly into the heart of the AI computing architecture, Marvell is fundamentally redesigning how AI data centers are built and operated, paving the way for the robust and demanding AI applications of tomorrow.

What's Next for AI Data Center Infrastructure

The unveiling of Marvell's 2nm optical technology marks a significant milestone, but it is merely a step in the ongoing journey of advancing AI data center infrastructure. The implications of this technology will undoubtedly ripple across the entire tech ecosystem. We can anticipate broader adoption of similar high-speed, energy-efficient optical interconnects as other industry players strive to match these capabilities. This will likely accelerate the development of even more powerful AI models and applications, impacting everything from autonomous systems and advanced scientific research to more sophisticated online productivity tools and real-time content generation platforms.

The continuous innovation in foundational hardware, such as Marvell's optical solutions, will remain critical to sustaining the rapid growth of AI. As AI becomes increasingly pervasive, the demand for underlying infrastructure that can handle immense computational loads with minimal latency and power consumption will only intensify. Companies like Marvell are laying the essential groundwork, ensuring that the promise of artificial intelligence can be fully realized through robust, scalable, and sustainable data center solutions for years to come.
