Anthropic nears $1 trillion valuation, plans wider Mythos release

Anthropic's valuation approached $1 trillion, passing OpenAI, as it announced plans to widen access to its Mythos-class models.

29 May 20264 min readBy Web Online Tools Editorial

In a seismic shift across the artificial intelligence landscape, Anthropic, the pioneering force behind the Claude family of chatbots, has achieved an astonishing valuation of $965 billion, officially leapfrogging rival OpenAI to become the world's most valuable AI startup. This monumental financial milestone, announced today, May 29, 2026, comes on the heels of a massive $65 billion fundraising round led by prominent private investors, including Altimeter Capital, Greenoaks, Dragoneer, and Sequoia Capital. More significantly, the company also revealed its ambitious plans to widely release its formidable "Mythos-level" AI models, previously deemed too hazardous for general public access, signaling a new era for powerful AI deployment with enhanced safety protocols.

The Unprecedented Rise of Anthropic in the AI Arena

Anthropic's meteoric ascent to a near-trillion-dollar valuation underscores the fervent investor confidence and surging demand for advanced artificial intelligence capabilities. The $65 billion capital injection catapults the San Francisco-based company, helmed by CEO and co-founder Dario Amodei, ahead of ChatGPT maker OpenAI, which secured an $852 billion valuation in its most recent funding round in March. This unprecedented increase in valuation for a startup highlights the intense competition and massive economic potential perceived within the frontier AI sector.

Analysts note that while publicly traded tech giants like Nvidia and Alphabet have seen even larger absolute increases, Anthropic's percentage growth in such a short period is truly exceptional. The company attributes this success to the historic demand for its products and its unwavering commitment to staying at the research frontier, promising to bring Claude to more platforms where work happens.

Mythos-Level AI: From Restricted to Ready for Broader Deployment

Perhaps the most impactful announcement from Anthropic today is its intention to widely release new artificial intelligence models in the coming weeks with cybersecurity capabilities comparable to "Mythos." Mythos, a technology previously described by Anthropic as too dangerous for general public availability, was initially limited to a handful of large tech and Wall Street companies through "Project Glasswing" due to its potent ability to identify and exploit vulnerabilities "in every major operating system and every major web browser."

Anthropic stated on Thursday, May 29, 2026, that it has made "swift progress" in developing "stronger safety safeguards" that will allow it to make these highly capable models accessible to all customers. This development is a crucial step for enterprise AI adoption, especially for critical applications like cybersecurity, where sophisticated AI can provide unparalleled defense mechanisms against evolving threats.

Enhanced Cybersecurity with Advanced AI Models

The practical implications of widely available Mythos-level AI are immense, particularly for cybersecurity. Japan's three megabanks – MUFG Bank, Sumitomo Mitsui Banking Corp., and Mizuho Bank – are already poised to gain access to OpenAI's new AI model, which is believed to have similar capabilities to Anthropic's Mythos, specifically to fortify their defenses against cyberattacks. These banks are also actively seeking direct access to Mythos to leverage both cutting-edge models for bolstering system security.

This demonstrates a clear industry trend: advanced AI models are becoming indispensable tools for detecting and neutralizing sophisticated digital threats, transforming cybersecurity from a reactive measure to a proactive, intelligent defense. For webonlinetools.com users, this highlights the growing importance of AI in securing digital assets and data, even if direct access to such models is primarily for enterprise clients initially.

Why This Matters for the AI Landscape and Beyond

Anthropic's latest announcements signify several critical shifts in the broader AI ecosystem. Firstly, it underscores the intensifying competition at the very top of the generative AI market. With Anthropic's valuation now exceeding OpenAI's, the race to develop and deploy more capable, yet safe, artificial general intelligence (AGI) continues to accelerate. This rivalry is driving rapid innovation, pushing the boundaries of what AI can achieve.

Secondly, the planned widespread release of Mythos-level AI models, coupled with new safety protocols, signals a maturing approach to AI development. Companies are recognizing the dual nature of powerful AI and are investing heavily in governance and safeguards to mitigate potential risks while maximizing beneficial applications. This is a positive sign for the responsible deployment of increasingly potent AI systems.

"This funding will help us serve the historic demand we are experiencing, stay at the research frontier, and bring Claude to more of the places where work happens," stated Anthropic's Chief Executive Officer Dario Amodei, emphasizing the company's commitment to growth and accessibility.

Furthermore, the focus on cybersecurity applications with these advanced models directly impacts productivity and stability across all digital sectors. Robust AI-powered security means fewer breaches, less downtime, and greater trust in online systems, which is paramount for businesses relying on web utilities and online tools.

What's Next for Anthropic and the Future of AI

Looking ahead, both Anthropic and OpenAI are expected to go public in the near future, anticipating some of the largest initial public offerings in history. This will provide further capital for research, development, and scaling of their formidable AI offerings. The continued evolution of models like Claude and Mythos will likely see even deeper integration into enterprise workflows, from software coding and content generation to complex data analysis and, crucially, robust cybersecurity defenses. As AI capabilities expand, we can expect a continued emphasis on developing transparent, controllable, and secure AI systems that empower users while minimizing risks, profoundly shaping the future of digital productivity and online tools.
