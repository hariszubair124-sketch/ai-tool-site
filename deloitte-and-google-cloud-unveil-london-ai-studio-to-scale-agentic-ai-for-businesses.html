Deloitte and Google Cloud open a London AI Studio

Published 17 June 2026 by Web Online Tools Editorial in Google

Deloitte and Google Cloud opened an AI Studio in London to help UK businesses build and deploy agentic AI.

In a significant move poised to reshape enterprise productivity and innovation, Deloitte and Google Cloud today announced the launch of a new state-of-the-art AI Studio on Deloitte's London campus. This collaborative initiative, unveiled at the Google Cloud London Summit 2026, marks a pivotal step in enabling British organizations to transition from experimental AI projects to the widespread deployment of autonomous, action-oriented AI systems, commonly known as Agentic AI. The studio aims to spearhead the UK's embrace of this advanced form of artificial intelligence, promising to drive real-world business outcomes and redefine digital workflows across a multitude of industries.

The Genesis of the London AI Studio

The newly established Deloitte AI Studio in London, developed in close partnership with Google Cloud, is designed as a co-innovation hub where Deloitte's experts and their clients will collaborate to build and deploy production-ready Agentic AI solutions. This strategic alliance underscores a shared vision to unlock the full potential of AI beyond mere data analysis or content generation, moving towards systems that can autonomously execute multi-step tasks and respond dynamically to complex business environments. The studio is scheduled to officially open its doors in late July 2026, setting the stage for a new era of AI-driven transformation within the UK business landscape.

What is Agentic AI?

Agentic AI represents the next frontier in artificial intelligence, distinguishing itself from earlier generative AI models by its capacity for autonomous action. While generative AI excels at creating content—be it text, images, or code—Agentic AI takes this a step further by acting as an "agent" capable of understanding goals, planning multi-step actions, executing those actions, and iterating on its approach based on feedback or new information. These systems are designed to perform complex transactional tasks, such as booking appointments, requesting quotes, or making reservations, essentially acting as digital colleagues. This evolution marks a shift from AI as a tool for information processing to AI as an active participant in operational processes.

Transforming Industries with Autonomous AI Solutions

The Deloitte AI Studio is set to focus its efforts on transitioning critical sectors that stand to gain immensely from advanced Agentic AI capabilities. Target industries include the public sector, financial services, retail, consumer products, healthcare and life sciences, and Technology, Media & Telecommunications (TMT). The goal is to move beyond simple conversational AI interfaces, which are common with generative models, to sophisticated systems that can modernize customer journeys and transform traditional business models and daily workflows.

  • Retail: Agentic AI solutions will shift from basic chat interfaces to sophisticated systems capable of automating complex customer interactions and optimizing supply chains.
  • Healthcare & Life Sciences: The studio will explore how Agentic AI can revolutionize operational efficiency, improve diagnostics, and personalize patient care, allowing AI to become a true lab assistant for researchers.
  • Financial Services: Automated compliance, fraud detection, and personalized financial advisory services powered by autonomous agents are anticipated.
  • Public Sector: Enhanced public service delivery through automated administrative tasks and intelligent resource allocation.

Hayley McKelvey, Chief AI Officer for Deloitte UK, emphasized this shift, stating, "Our clients are looking for more than just productivity gains—they want AI that can take action and drive real-world business outcomes." This sentiment highlights the growing demand for AI that acts as a proactive partner rather than just a reactive tool.

Deloitte and Google Cloud's Strategic Vision and Upskilling Initiative

Beyond the physical studio, Deloitte has also announced an intensive upskilling initiative. This program aims to train 1,000 members of its UK AI and data workforce on Google Cloud's Gemini Enterprise. This certification program will ensure that Deloitte's AI and data engineers possess the technical expertise required to implement Google's most advanced agentic architecture, creating one of the largest pools of certified AI talent in the region. This investment in human capital is crucial for successfully integrating and scaling complex Agentic AI solutions across various industries, reinforcing the UK's position as a leader in AI adoption.

What's Next for Agentic AI Adoption

The launch of the Deloitte and Google Cloud AI Studio is a clear indicator that Agentic AI is moving rapidly from conceptual exploration to tangible enterprise deployment. This shift is set to redefine how businesses operate, offering unprecedented opportunities for automation, efficiency, and strategic decision-making. As these autonomous systems become more prevalent, the focus will increasingly be on ensuring their ethical deployment, robust governance, and seamless integration into existing digital ecosystems.
