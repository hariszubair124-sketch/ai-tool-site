GPT-4o two years later: how businesses use it

Two years after its release, OpenAI's GPT-4o is still widely used in business. How companies use its multimodal features in 2026.

16 May 20265 min readBy Web Online Tools Editorial

Introduction: The Persistent Influence of Conversational AI

May 16, 2026, marks a significant point for reflection in the world of artificial intelligence. Two years ago, OpenAI unveiled GPT-4o – with "o" standing for "omni" – a groundbreaking multimodal large language model that promised to revolutionize human-computer interaction by seamlessly integrating text, audio, and vision capabilities. Today, as we look back, GPT-4o's influence on enterprise productivity and the broader digital landscape is more evident than ever. Despite the continuous evolution of AI models, including the emergence of successors, GPT-4o's foundational breakthroughs continue to power a myriad of applications, particularly within specialized business contexts and integrated online tools, demonstrating a lasting impact that shapes how we work and interact with technology.

This enduring relevance stems from its unparalleled ability to process and generate content across diverse modalities in real time, setting a new benchmark for accessible and natural AI interactions. The model, developed by OpenAI, quickly became a cornerstone for developers and businesses worldwide, offering advanced capabilities that transcended simple text generation. Its introduction fundamentally altered expectations for what conversational AI could achieve, fostering innovation in areas from customer service to content creation and coding assistance. The impact of such multimodal AI continues to be a central theme in tech discussions, influencing strategic decisions across industries.

GPT-4o's Core Multimodal Innovations and Continued Relevance

At its core, GPT-4o distinguished itself by being a single neural network capable of processing any combination of text, image, and audio inputs and responding with outputs in any of those forms. This was a significant leap from previous models that often required separate components for different data types. This unified approach led to several key innovations that remain highly pertinent today:

Real-time Multimodal Processing

One of GPT-4o's most celebrated features was its ability to engage in real-time verbal conversations with human-like response times, averaging around 320 milliseconds – remarkably close to the average human response time of 210 milliseconds. This low latency transformed interactions, making AI assistants feel more natural and intuitive. This capability has been particularly valuable in live support systems and interactive learning environments, where seamless back-and-forth communication is crucial. Even as newer models emerge, the real-time audio and video processing, particularly in specialized voice modes, remains a powerful testament to GPT-4o's design.

Advanced Language and Sentiment Understanding

Beyond speed, GPT-4o showcased advanced capabilities in handling over 50 different languages, significantly improving speech recognition and translation, especially for lower-resourced languages. Furthermore, the model could understand user sentiment across text, audio, and video modalities, and even generate speech with emotional nuances. This sophisticated understanding of context and emotion allows for more empathetic and effective AI interactions, proving invaluable in applications requiring sensitive and nuanced communication, such as mental health support or personalized tutoring.

Accessibility and Inclusivity through AI

GPT-4o made significant strides in accessibility. Its ability to describe visual content for individuals with visual impairments and offer real-time translation broke down barriers, making technology more inclusive. This omni-modal approach ensures that users with diverse needs can interact with AI and digital content more effectively, fostering a more equitable digital environment. This commitment to accessibility continues to be a driving force in many online tool development initiatives today.

Transforming Workflows: GPT-4o in Business and Productivity Tools

The impact of GPT-4o on business operations and productivity tools has been profound and continues to evolve. Its versatility allows for integration into numerous workflows, streamlining tasks and enhancing efficiency across various sectors. For webonlinetools.com, these advancements represent a continuous source of inspiration for developing and curating cutting-edge online utilities.

  • Enhanced Customer Service: Businesses leverage GPT-4o's multimodal capabilities to power more dynamic and comprehensive customer support. Real-time translation and sentiment analysis enable AI agents to provide personalized and efficient assistance, even across language barriers.
  • Advanced Analytics and Reporting: GPT-4o can analyze complex datasets, generate visualizations like charts, and produce detailed reports, significantly improving data-driven decision-making processes for executives and managers.
  • Creative Content Generation: From drafting marketing copy and social media updates to generating code snippets and even musical compositions, GPT-4o continues to be a powerful engine for creative content, assisting professionals in generating diverse formats quickly and efficiently.
  • Software Development and Debugging: Developers integrate GPT-4o into their environments to generate code, suggest improvements, and debug errors, substantially reducing development time and improving code quality. This augments the capabilities of numerous online coding and text editing tools.
  • Personalized Virtual Assistants: The model's ability to maintain continuity across conversations and understand emotional nuances has led to the creation of more cohesive and personalized virtual assistants, improving user experience across various platforms.

According to reports from its initial rollout, GPT-4o was designed to be approximately 50% cheaper than its predecessor, GPT-4 Turbo, for API usage, making advanced AI more accessible for widespread implementation. This cost-efficiency, combined with its advanced capabilities, has cemented its place in numerous enterprise solutions, proving that powerful AI can also be economically viable.

Why This Matters: A Blueprint for Future AI Integration

GPT-4o's lasting significance lies not just in its individual capabilities but in how it served as a blueprint for truly integrated multimodal AI. Its release marked a paradigm shift, demonstrating that AI could move beyond specialized tasks to offer a holistic, human-like interaction experience. Sam Altman, OpenAI's CEO, notably described GPT-4o's voice and video mode as the “best computer interface I've ever used” upon its initial announcement. This vision of a seamlessly conversational and perceptive AI continues to drive innovation today.

The model’s robust API access and the introduction of corporate customization options in August 2024 allowed businesses to fine-tune GPT-4o with their proprietary data, enabling highly specialized and efficient applications. This focus on practical, adaptable AI solutions has democratized access to advanced capabilities, allowing even smaller businesses and developers to leverage sophisticated AI for competitive advantage. GPT-4o proved that advanced AI could be both powerful and broadly accessible, setting a high bar for future developments in the field.

What's Next: The Evolving Role of Foundational Multimodal Models

As we navigate further into 2026, the landscape of AI continues its rapid evolution. While OpenAI has since introduced newer flagship models like GPT-5, the core innovations of GPT-4o remain deeply embedded in many critical systems and applications. Its multimodal engine, particularly for real-time voice and vision interactions, continues to power specialized features within ChatGPT and other platforms, especially for paid subscribers. The emphasis will likely shift towards greater efficiency, enhanced reasoning capabilities, and even more seamless integration into daily workflows, perhaps through smaller, highly optimized versions like GPT-4o mini, which was released in July 2024. Expect further refinements in fine-tuning capabilities, enabling even deeper customization for industry-specific challenges. The ongoing commitment to making advanced AI accessible and beneficial to everyone, as espoused by OpenAI, will continue to drive the development of these foundational multimodal models, ensuring their enduring impact on how we interact with the digital world and enhance our productivity.
