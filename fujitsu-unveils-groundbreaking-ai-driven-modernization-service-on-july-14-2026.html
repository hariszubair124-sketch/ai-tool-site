Fujitsu launches an AI service for modernising legacy systems

Fujitsu launched an AI-driven Modernization Service on 14 July 2026 that uses generative AI to help companies update legacy systems.

14 July 20264 min readBy Web Online Tools Editorial

On July 14, 2026, Fujitsu Limited announced the Japan launch of its highly anticipated Fujitsu AI-driven Modernization Service, a significant leap forward in enterprise digital and AI transformation. This new service promises to revolutionize how businesses handle their complex legacy systems by integrating cutting-edge generative AI with Fujitsu's decades of system integration expertise. The initiative is set to support the sustainable growth of businesses and organizations, particularly those grappling with the urgent need to modernize their foundational IT infrastructure to remain competitive in a rapidly evolving digital landscape.

The Dawn of AI-Powered Legacy System Transformation

The imperative for modernization has never been more critical, especially with the accelerated pace of digital transformation (DX) and AI transformation (AX) across industries. Sectors like finance, public services, and healthcare, which frequently face regulatory revisions, alongside manufacturing and distribution, with their complex operational management built on years of legacy systems, are under immense pressure to adapt. Fujitsu's new service directly addresses these challenges by automating and optimizing modernization initiatives, primarily focusing on rewrite and rehost approaches.

This service combines Fujitsu's proprietary AI platform, Fujitsu Kozuchi, and its Takane large language model (LLM), with advanced AI technologies from industry leaders like Anthropic PBC's Claude and OpenAI's GPT. The synergy of these AI models, coupled with the practical knowledge of Fujitsu's specialized engineers, known as "Modernization Meisters," enables a speedy and high-quality transformation. This integrated approach can potentially shorten migration periods by approximately 40%.

How the Fujitsu AI-driven Modernization Service Works

At its core, the service leverages AI-driven automation to accelerate modernization. It comprehensively analyzes various data from target legacy assets, centralizing them as AI-ready structured data through Fujitsu's proprietary AI technology. This process streamlines the identification of core information for modernization.

  • AI-Driven Automation: The service uses AI to analyze legacy system data and manage it as structured data, crucial for modernization.
  • Multi-AI Modernization: By optimally utilizing multiple AI models, including Fujitsu Kozuchi, Takane LLM, Anthropic's Claude, and OpenAI's GPT, the service promotes customer DX.
  • Dedicated AI Agents: Specialized AI agents, trained on Fujitsu's extensive project data and knowledge base, ensure high-speed and high-precision modernization.
  • Self-Service Platforms: Fujitsu plans to sequentially provide self-service AI platforms, empowering customers to perform modernization tasks themselves, leveraging the practical insights gained from this service.

The service is designed to free customers from the burden of optimally selecting and operating rapidly evolving AI technologies, allowing them to focus on core initiatives like business transformation and maximizing ROI.

The Broader Impact and Future Implications of AI Agents

The move towards more autonomous AI agents, as demonstrated by Fujitsu's new service and other industry trends, is a major theme in July 2026. Experts indicate that the industry is shifting from chasing raw model size to optimizing for usefulness, cost, and reliability. What's Next for AI-Driven Modernization

The launch of the Fujitsu AI-driven Modernization Service is a clear indicator of the direction AI is heading – towards deeper integration, enhanced autonomy, and a focus on solving complex, real-world business challenges. As Fujitsu plans to roll out self-service platforms, the accessibility of these sophisticated AI tools will empower an even broader range of organizations to take control of their modernization journeys.
