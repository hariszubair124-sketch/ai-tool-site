Apple announces foldable iPhone and iPhone 18

At its Surprise and Shine event on 9 September 2026, Apple showed its first foldable iPhone and the iPhone 18 series under CEO John Ternus.

9 September 20264 min readBy Web Online Tools Editorial

Cupertino, California – September 9, 2026, marks a pivotal day in consumer technology as Apple hosted its much-anticipated "Surprise and Shine" event from its iconic Apple Park headquarters. Under the leadership of its new CEO, John Ternus, the tech giant introduced a suite of groundbreaking devices, headlined by the company's first-ever foldable iPhone and the next-generation iPhone 18 Pro series. This event signals a bold new direction for Apple, embracing innovative form factors and pushing the boundaries of mobile computing and design.

For years, the technology world has buzzed with speculation about Apple's entry into the foldable smartphone market. Today, that speculation culminated in a reality that promises to reshape mobile productivity and user interaction. The unveiling demonstrates Apple's commitment to innovation, even as other manufacturers have been in the foldable space for some time.

The Dawn of the Apple Foldable iPhone: iPhone Ultra Emerges

The centerpiece of today's "Surprise and Shine" event was undoubtedly the debut of Apple's first foldable phone, widely rumored to be dubbed the iPhone Ultra. This highly anticipated device represents a significant leap for Apple, introducing a virtually creaseless, passport-like form factor that promises an expansive display when unfolded, offering unprecedented screen real estate for multitasking, content creation, and immersive entertainment. Industry analysts, such as Dan Ives, a partner and analyst at Yorkville Ives & Co., believe that the foldable iPhone could be a major hit for Apple, asserting that "this is the right time to go after the new hardware chapter under Ternus."

Apple's strategic entry into the foldable segment is expected to invigorate a market that, while innovative, has yet to see widespread adoption in certain regions like the U.S. With competitors such as Samsung and Huawei having offered foldable devices for years, Apple's move is poised to significantly boost the overall foldable device sales, with IDC estimating an increase of 13% this year, reaching 22.9 million units. This new design paradigm is not just about a larger screen; it's about reimagining how users interact with their digital tools, offering greater flexibility and enhancing productivity on the go. The ability to switch seamlessly between a compact phone and a tablet-sized display could revolutionize how we utilize web utilities, text editors, and other online productivity tools.

Introducing the Powerful iPhone 18 Pro Series

Beyond the groundbreaking foldable, Apple also showcased its formidable iPhone 18 Pro series, comprising the iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max. These new flagship smartphones are engineered to deliver unparalleled performance and photographic capabilities. Both models are expected to feature Apple's cutting-edge A20 Pro chip, manufactured on a 2nm process, ensuring blazing-fast processing speeds and enhanced energy efficiency. Users can anticipate significant camera upgrades, promising more sophisticated photography and videography experiences, alongside a refined design that includes a smaller Dynamic Island and potential changes to the Camera Control button.

The iPhone 18 Pro Max, in particular, is rumored to boast an even larger and more efficient battery, aiming to provide the best battery life ever seen in an iPhone. These advancements underscore Apple's continuous pursuit of excellence in core smartphone functionalities, catering to power users who demand the very best in terms of speed, camera quality, and endurance. For professionals and casual users alike, the iPhone 18 Pro series represents a significant upgrade, further cementing its role as a powerful tool for communication, creation, and consumption.

A New Era Under CEO John Ternus

Today's "Surprise and Shine" event held particular significance as it marked the first major product launch under Apple's new CEO, John Ternus, who succeeded longtime leader Tim Cook on September 1. Ternus's debut on stage to introduce such monumental products as the foldable iPhone and the iPhone 18 series signals a confident new chapter for Apple, demonstrating a willingness to explore new territories while maintaining the company's renowned commitment to design and user experience. The event commenced at 1 p.m. ET at Apple's Cupertino headquarters, captivating a global audience. This strategic unveiling before the devices hit shelves, typically a few days later, builds anticipation and sets the stage for a strong holiday season.

Why This Matters for Digital Productivity and Online Tools

For the audience of webonlinetools.com, these new Apple devices carry immense implications for digital productivity and the use of online tools. The Apple Foldable iPhone, with its expanded screen real estate, offers an ideal platform for multitasking across web-based applications, enabling users to manage documents, spreadsheets, and creative projects with desktop-like efficiency on a portable device. Imagine editing complex text documents, converting files, or utilizing advanced web utilities side-by-side without compromising usability. The larger display facilitates more intuitive interaction with productivity suites and collaborative platforms.

Furthermore, the enhanced processing power of the iPhone 18 Pro series, driven by the A20 Pro chip, means online tools will run smoother, faster, and more responsively than ever before. This is crucial for resource-intensive web applications, AI-powered productivity tools, and complex web utilities that demand robust computing capabilities. As AI continues to integrate into everyday software and services, these powerful devices will be essential for harnessing the full potential of next-generation online tools, ensuring that webonlinetools.com users can maximize their efficiency and creative output.

What's Next for Apple and the Mobile Landscape

The launch of the Apple Foldable iPhone and the iPhone 18 Pro series is not merely about new products; it's about setting the trajectory for the next phase of mobile technology. Apple's entry into the foldable market will undoubtedly intensify competition, spurring further innovation from rivals and accelerating the development of more refined and accessible foldable devices. This could lead to a rapid evolution in mobile operating systems and application design, optimizing experiences for dynamic screen real estate. As these devices become available, consumers can look forward to unprecedented levels of mobile productivity, entertainment, and connectivity, further blurring the lines between smartphones and tablets and solidifying the role of powerful, versatile mobile devices in our daily digital lives. The influence of today’s announcements will resonate across the tech industry for years to come.
