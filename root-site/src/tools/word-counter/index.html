The Essential Text Analytics Tool

Whether you are drafting ad copy with strict length restrictions, preparing an essay with a minimum word limit, or optimizing meta tags for SEO performance, a reliable analytics tool is indispensable. Our application provides immediate, client-side metrics without sending your proprietary content to a remote server.

Advanced Metrics Explained

  • Word & Character Limits: Crucial for digital marketers navigating character constraints on platforms like X (Twitter), Google Search snippets, and SMS campaigns. Our tool properly accounts for apostrophes and hyphenated words, ensuring accurate counts.
  • Reading Time Estimation: Evaluates your content against the industry-standard reading speed of ~225 words per minute. This metric is incredibly useful for content creators aiming for specific engagement targets on blogs or Medium articles.
  • Keyword Density (SEO): Automatically strips out common English stop words (like "the", "and", "is") and ranks your most frequently used terms. This ensures your copy remains highly relevant to search intent without falling into keyword stuffing.
