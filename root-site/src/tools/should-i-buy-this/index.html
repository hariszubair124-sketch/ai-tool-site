Making Smart Financial Decisions

This cost benefit calculator helps you justify a purchase by framing its cost against time and opportunity. It is an essential tool for smart spending, frugal living, and improving your personal finance mindset. By calculating the ROI of a purchase in terms of time, you remove emotional impulse and look purely at the data.

Understanding the Metrics

  • Your Hourly Value: Your time is your most finite resource. By assigning a dollar value to your hour, you can quantify "convenience".
  • Break-Even Point: If you buy a $400 robot vacuum that saves you 1 hour a week (valued at $30/hr), it takes roughly 13 weeks to "pay for itself" in time.
  • If invested instead: Every purchase carries an opportunity cost. If you didn't buy this item, and instead invested the money in an index fund yielding a conservative 7% annual return, how much would you have at the end of the item's lifespan? This metric puts luxury purchases into stark perspective.
